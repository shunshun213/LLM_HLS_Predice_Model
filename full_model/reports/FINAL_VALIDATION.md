**Risk for the hidden test set.** The `mixed` group (99 of 216 train designs, random combinations of every pragma) is the most test-like. Its LUT/FF error is the largest and grows when its own designs are held out of the Stage 2 fit (see the table). Designs that combine several reshape / partition / storage pragmas, or that trigger a low-confidence rule (reported by `predict.py` under `confidence`), should be expected to carry LUT / FF errors above 20 %, with a tendency to under-predict (negative bias on `mixed`, table above); latency, DSP and BRAM are structural counts and generalize much better.

- designs with a confidence flag (76): LUT MAPE 17.9 %, FF MAPE 18.9 %
- designs without (140): LUT MAPE 9.9 %, FF MAPE 10.1 %

## 6. Robustness over the design space

5000 random pragma sets over the whole Target design space (pipeline on none / k / j / i, unroll factors incl. non-divisors of 64, every partition / reshape / storage / bind_op option, 4 clocks), each rendered as a Tcl script and as a C++ source with inline pragmas and parsed back through `stage1_parse`: 5000 identical round trips with finite predictions, 0 failures. `test_parser.py` covers non-canonical syntax and out-of-space input (clean `UnsupportedDesign` errors).
